OUR STORY

About Rizwana Bakery

Rizwana Bakery is a cherished local bakery located in Sector 11 E Muhammad Nagar, Karachi, Pakistan. We are dedicated to offering a warm, welcoming experience for anyone seeking high-quality baked goods for their daily enjoyment or special family gatherings.

Our selection includes a wide array of bakery products ranging from rich celebration cakes and delicate pastries to soft daily breads, crisp biscuits, and delectable sweet treats. Whether you are dropping in for morning staples or planning a sweet addition to a celebration, Rizwana Bakery offers delicious bakery concepts crafted with care.
